Terms of use and responsible engagement

The Isingo Advocates for Humanity website exists to inform the public, support access to justice and share information about our work in Uganda.

Visitors are expected to use this website responsibly and respectfully. Content on the site is provided for informational and advocacy purposes and should not be treated as legal advice unless specifically communicated in that context.

We welcome appropriate engagement, constructive feedback and requests for support. However, we reserve the right to restrict activity that is abusive, unlawful, disruptive or inconsistent with the dignity and safety of the communities we serve.
