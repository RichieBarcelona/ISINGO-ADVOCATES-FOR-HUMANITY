Internship & volunteer

Get involved

Internship opportunities

The organisation provides internship opportunities for international students and volunteers from around the world. These opportunities are meant to support practical engagement with rights-based, humanitarian and educational work in Uganda.

Volunteer opportunities

People interested in supporting the organisation’s work may express their interest in volunteering. This is a general call for engagement and should be followed up through direct contact.

Apply / Express Interest

Legal and advocacy support

Opportunities may involve support in legal research, advocacy, outreach and rights-based work in line with organisational priorities.

Humanitarian service

Volunteers may contribute to humanitarian and community-focused work that supports vulnerable persons and strengthens local response.

Education and outreach

Support may involve education, community learning and engagement that promotes rights awareness and public participation.

Isingo Advocates for Humanity

Independent, not-for-profit and non-partisan. Working in Uganda to protect civil liberties, defend human rights and improve access to humanitarian and educational support.
