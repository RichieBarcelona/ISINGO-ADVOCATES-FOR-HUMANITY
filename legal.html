Legal services

Our legal response

Protecting rights through advice, advocacy and legal action

Legal advice

We provide pro-bono legal advice and guidance to help people understand their rights, options and appropriate next steps.

Legal representation

We provide legal representation for eligible persons and cases where appropriate, with attention to rights, fairness and access to justice.

Mediation

Mediation is used as an alternative dispute-resolution mechanism when appropriate, helping parties address disputes through dialogue and structured negotiation.

Arbitration

Arbitration may be used as an alternative dispute-resolution mechanism where appropriate, particularly where a formal legal process may not be the most suitable first route.

Litigation

Where necessary, we engage in litigation and public-interest litigation to challenge abuse and protect civil liberties and human rights.

Research & legal analysis

We build legal understanding through research, analysis and careful review of rights issues, laws, policies and their effects on communities.

Advocacy

Our advocacy seeks to influence laws, policies and practices so that civil liberties and rights are protected in a fair and accountable system.

Important principle

Alternative dispute resolution receives first priority where appropriate

The organisation prioritises mediation and arbitration as alternative dispute-resolution mechanisms where appropriate, before moving to more formal legal processes. This approach supports timely, proportionate and rights-based responses.

How we work

Our legal work is both proactive and reactive: we respond to immediate rights violations and also engage in wider research, advocacy and public-interest actions to strengthen the legal environment.
