Privacy policy

Privacy

How we handle your information

Isingo Advocates for Humanity is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who contact us for legal, humanitarian, education or advocacy support.

We collect only the information needed to assess an inquiry, respond appropriately and protect the safety, dignity and rights of the people who reach out to us. This may include contact details, location information, case information and the nature of the support requested.

We use personal information strictly for the purpose for which it was provided, to coordinate support, maintain records and communicate with relevant parties. We do not sell personal data and we limit access to information to authorised staff or partners working under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

Contact

If you have a privacy concern, need to correct your information or want to understand how a request was handled, please contact Isingo through the contact page or by email at greenhillnps@gmail.com.
