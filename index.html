Defending Rights. Promoting Dignity. Serving Humanity.

Isingo Advocates for Humanity is an independent, not-for-profit and non-partisan organisation dedicated to the protection of civil liberties, the promotion of human rights, and the provision of educational and humanitarian services.

Who we are

An independent organisation for rights, dignity and service

Formed in 2019 and incorporated in 2022 under the laws of Uganda, Isingo Advocates for Humanity works to promote and defend human rights, strengthen humanitarian services, and provide educational opportunities to poor and vulnerable persons.

  • Promote human rights
  • Defend civil liberties
  • Strengthen humanitarian services
  • Provide educational opportunities
  • Support vulnerable and marginalized persons

Three focus areas

Our work is organised around Legal, Humanitarian and Education services. We combine a proactive and reactive approach, using research, advocacy, outreach, legal analysis and, where appropriate, litigation.

What we do

Legal, humanitarian and education work

These three areas guide how we respond to abuse of civil liberties and human rights, and how we serve communities in need.

Legal

A legal response to the abuse of civil liberties and human rights, with alternative dispute resolution given first priority where appropriate.

  • Legal advice
  • Legal representation
  • Mediation and arbitration
  • Litigation
  • Legal research and advocacy
Legal services

Humanitarian

Humanitarian assistance and community support for people in vulnerable situations, including sick persons, elderly persons and children.

  • Assistance to vulnerable persons
  • Support to sick persons
  • Support to elderly persons
  • Support to children
  • Community support
Humanitarian work

Education

Educational opportunities for poor and vulnerable persons, together with support to children, schools and human-rights education.

  • Educational opportunities
  • Support to children
  • Support to schools
  • Community education
  • Human-rights education
Education work

Our mission

Mission

To provide a robust, strategic and non-discriminatory legal and humanitarian response to the abuse of civil liberties and human rights in Uganda, together with public-interest litigation, research, legal analysis and advocacy.

Our vision

Vision

“A society in which civil liberties and human rights are enjoyed without discrimination and those who abuse the rights are brought to account.”
Isingo Advocates for Humanity

Commitments that guide our work

Independence & impartiality

Research & advocacy
